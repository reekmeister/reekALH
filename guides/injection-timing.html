Injection Timing with VCDS

Use this after timing-belt work or when checking VE-pump timing.

Workflow

  1. Warm the engine so coolant temperature is above 85°C.
  2. Connect VCDS and enter 01-Engine.
  3. Open Measuring Blocks and select Group 000, then switch to Basic Settings.
  4. Open the TDI Timing checker and select the ALH/appropriate VE engine.
  5. Verify that the timing point is within the displayed specification band; save a screenshot or log before and after any adjustment.

Important note

Ross-Tech specifically says this procedure applies to VE engines and not PD or common-rail engines.
