ALH REFERENCE GUIDE

MAF Check — Measuring Block 003

A quick diagnostic starting point for reduced-power complaints.

Workflow

  1. Connect VCDS and enter 01-Engine.
  2. Open Measuring Block 003 and log the relevant fields.
  3. Under load in a high gear, compare actual MAF against requested/expected behavior.
  4. Inspect air filter, intake tract, EGR/intake deposits and electrical connections before condemning the sensor.
  5. Use the factory procedure and vehicle-specific limits when deciding whether a component is failed.

Important note

Ross-Tech describes a stock 90-hp A4 TDI ALH MAF check using measuring block 003 and notes that actual MAF should reach at least about 850–900 mg/h at 3000 rpm under its stated test conditions.
