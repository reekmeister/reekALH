Glow-Plug Diagnosis

A basic electrical workflow for hard cold starts.

Workflow

  1. Check battery condition and cranking speed first.
  2. Measure glow-plug resistance using an appropriate meter and wiring configuration.
  3. Inspect the harness and common power/ground paths.
  4. Check the glow-plug control circuit and relay/fuse information for the specific vehicle.
  5. A glow-plug fault does not automatically explain every hard-start complaint; check fuel supply, injection timing and compression as needed.

Important note

Do not power-test glow plugs in a way that can damage the harness or controller. Follow the applicable factory electrical test.
