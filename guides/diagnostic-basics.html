ALH REFERENCE GUIDE

ALH Diagnostic Workflow

A repeatable path for most driveability complaints.

Workflow

  1. Record symptoms, DTCs and freeze-frame or live-data information before changing parts.
  2. Determine whether the complaint is fuel, air/boost, mechanical timing, electrical or transmission-related.
  3. Check simple causes first: hoses, connectors, fuses, grounds, filters and vacuum plumbing.
  4. Use VCDS measuring blocks to test the component under the conditions where the fault appears.
  5. Only then replace parts, and re-test the system to confirm the repair.

Important note

This site is a reference index. For safety-critical work and exact torque/sequence values, use the vehicle-specific factory/Bentley repair information.
