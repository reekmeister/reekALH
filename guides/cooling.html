ALH REFERENCE GUIDE

Cooling System Diagnostic Workflow

Use this as a troubleshooting tree, not as a substitute for the factory repair sequence.

Workflow

  1. Check coolant level and visible leaks with the engine cold.
  2. Inspect the coolant flange, hoses, radiator and water-pump area.
  3. Verify coolant-temperature sensor behavior with scan data and gauge behavior.
  4. Check thermostat operation if warm-up is abnormal.
  5. After any coolant-system repair, refill and bleed according to the vehicle/factory procedure and confirm stable operating temperature.

Important note

Model year and body style can change component details. Verify the vehicle-specific factory procedure before disassembly.
