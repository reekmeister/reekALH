ALH REFERENCE GUIDE

Boost / VNT Check — Measuring Block 011

A practical starting workflow for underboost, overboost and limp-mode complaints.

Workflow

  1. Inspect vacuum hoses and the N75 system first.
  2. Connect VCDS and log Measuring Block 011.
  3. Under controlled full-load conditions, compare specified/requested boost with actual boost.
  4. Inspect the VNT actuator movement and mechanical linkage for binding.
  5. Only after the mechanical and vacuum checks should you condemn the turbocharger or N75.

Important note

Ross-Tech uses Group 011 to compare actual and requested boost on stock TDI applications.
